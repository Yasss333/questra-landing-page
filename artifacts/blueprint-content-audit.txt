03 / Why Questra
Move beyond fragmented examination workflows.
Manual paper setting often involves disconnected spreadsheets, inconsistent difficulty, repeated questions, slow approvals and limited visibility into syllabus coverage. Questra is designed to connect academic data, question creation, validation, blueprint rules and review into one transparent process.
Value pillars: Reduce repetitive preparation • Improve academic consistency • Strengthen oversight • Reveal coverage gaps
04 / AI Question Studio
From a syllabus topic to a faculty-ready question.
Select a degree, department, subject, unit and topic. Set the number of questions, marks, question type, difficulty and Bloom’s level. Preview AI-generated drafts, refine wording and answers, regenerate alternatives, review quality signals and submit approved content to the Question Bank.
Screen 1: Topic-based generation | Screen 2: Faculty question editor | Screen 3: Review and approval
05 / Academic Master Data
Every examination starts with connected academic data.
Maintain institutions, academic years, batches, degrees, departments, semesters, subject types, subjects, students and staff. Support spreadsheet imports, validation, data completeness monitoring and faculty-to-subject mapping.
Student Master: registration number, name, sex, DOB, contact, photo, batch/degree/department/semester. Staff Master: profile, department, designation, multiple subject assignments and examination roles.
06 / Syllabus Intelligence
Make every question traceable to the curriculum.
Upload and view syllabus PDFs. Structure units, topics and subtopics, map them to subjects and academic years, connect Course Outcomes and define mid-semester or end-semester scope. Surface missing mappings and areas with insufficient approved questions.
Key capabilities: PDF viewing • Topic hierarchy • Syllabus versioning • Exam-scope mapping • Coverage gap alerts
07 / Unified Question Bank
One governed repository for every question.
Create questions manually or through AI assistance. Organize MCQs, short answers, essays, numerical and case-based questions with marks, difficulty, Bloom’s levels, Course Outcomes, answers and rubrics. Search, filter, edit, archive and review question histories.
Key capabilities: Structured metadata • Duplicate detection • Approval status • Question variants • Audit history
08 / Paper Blueprint & Pattern Studio
Design the examination before generating the paper.
Create reusable templates for duration, maximum marks, sections, compulsory and optional questions, internal choices, unit weightage, difficulty mix, cognitive levels and outcome coverage. Validate blueprint totals and institution-specific rules.
Key capabilities: Part A/B/C layouts • Either/or choices • Marks validation • Reusable templates
09 / Autonomous Paper Generation
Generate to the blueprint. Keep academics in control.
Assemble draft examination papers from approved questions using configurable syllabus, difficulty, Bloom’s, marks and outcome constraints. Review alternatives, replace questions, compare paper sets and export approved formats.
Key capabilities: Set A/B/C/D • Repetition checks • Preview • Authorized substitutions • Word/PDF output
10 / Academic Quality Intelligence
More than generation: structured quality review.
Design checks for syllabus relevance, duplicate meaning, question clarity, answer correctness, Bloom’s consistency, MCQ validity, numerical reasoning and blueprint compliance. Quality signals support reviewers; they do not replace academic verification.
Key capabilities: Semantic similarity • Ambiguity alerts • Answer review • Constraint checks
11 / Faculty Review & Approval
Every decision accountable. Every revision traceable.
Route questions and papers through faculty editing, reviewer comments, moderation, resubmission and final authorization. Preserve original AI drafts, revisions, approval records, timestamps and release controls.
Workflow: Draft → Edited → Submitted → Reviewed → Approved → Locked / Released
12 / Coverage, Bloom’s & OBE Analytics
Know what the paper measures—not just what it contains.
Explore question availability by subject, unit and topic. Compare planned and actual Bloom’s K1–K6 distribution. Map questions to Course Outcomes and Programme Outcomes and highlight weak coverage before papers are finalized.
Key capabilities: Coverage Explorer • Cognitive distribution • CO/PO matrices • Marks-weighted analysis
13 / Command Center & Reports
One clear view of examination readiness.
Give administrators visibility into master-data completeness, question inventory, AI/manual contribution, faculty workload, pending reviews, paper readiness, coverage gaps and examination deadlines. Enable filtered exports for departmental and institutional review.
Key capabilities: Executive dashboards • Department drill-down • Alerts • Excel/CSV/PDF reporting
14 / Requests, Formats & Governance
Built for coordinated examination teams.
Manage paper requests, assigned setters, deadlines, review queues and release approvals. Configure institution-specific headers, instructions, numbering and page layouts. Design role-based permissions, controlled access, version history and audit trails.
Key capabilities: Task queues • Branded formats • Access control • Release safeguards

20 / FAQ Copy
Answer the questions institutions ask first.

Can existing data be imported? Structured Excel/CSV imports are part of the design. Does Questra support OBE? Planned CO/PO mapping and Bloom’s analytics support OBE-oriented review.

27 / Student Question Bank — Practice and Discovery
Question types  MCQ, very short answer, short answer, descriptive, numerical, problem solving, case study and application-based questions, subject to academic configuration.
Metadata  Question ID, subject, unit, topic, question type, marks, difficulty, Bloom’s level, CO, publication status and optional source/reference.
Search and discovery  Keyword search; filters by subject, unit, topic, type, marks, difficulty, Bloom’s K1–K6 and CO; sort by topic, marks or recently published.
Question detail  Full question, diagrams/equations where applicable, optional hint, published model answer and marking guidance only where faculty policy allows.
Student experience  Bookmark, organize revision lists and record practice completion as a later phase. Avoid exposing secure upcoming-exam questions or staff-only bank items.
Sales copy  Practice with purpose. Every published question carries academic context—not just an answer.
28 / Previous Examination Papers — Mid-Term and End-Semester
Paper library  Separate Mid-Term I, Mid-Term II and End-Semester papers; configurable local naming and examination categories.
Metadata  Institution, subject code/title, exam type, academic year, semester, exam date, duration, maximum marks, paper set/version and release status.
Search and filters  Subject, exam type, academic year, semester, date and paper set; show only institution-authorized past papers.
Viewer  Embedded PDF-style viewer with zoom, page navigation, print/download only when permitted, and accessible alternative document download.
Publication governance  Release after examination completion and authorized approval; respect embargo, copyright, retention and access controls. Never expose unreleased paper sets or confidential marking schemes.
Sales copy  Learn from the questions that shaped previous examinations—with a structured, searchable archive of officially released papers.
29 / Bloom’s Taxonomy and Outcome Analytics for Students
Bloom’s insights  Show distribution of available published questions across K1 Remember, K2 Understand, K3 Apply, K4 Analyze, K5 Evaluate and K6 Create; support count and marks-weighted views.
Outcome insights  Show Course Outcomes CO1–CO5 or configured equivalents, mapped questions, marks bands and coverage by unit.
Analytics distinction  Question availability, syllabus coverage and CO/Bloom’s distribution are content analytics. Student proficiency, readiness or mastery requires actual student attempt/assessment evidence and must be separately labeled.
Visualizations  Question type doughnut, Bloom’s bar chart, CO coverage bars, unit heatmap, topic question count table and drill-down filters.
Sales copy  See beyond question counts. Understand the thinking skills and learning outcomes represented in your preparation resources.

33 / Student Data Model and API Integration
Entities  Student, Enrollment, AcademicYear, Batch, Degree, Department, Semester, Subject, CurriculumVersion, SyllabusDocument, Unit, Topic, Subtopic, CourseOutcome, BloomLevel, Question, QuestionPublication, Exam, Paper, PaperVersion, PaperRelease, StudentBookmark, AuditEvent.
Relationships  Student → Enrollment → SubjectOffering; SubjectOffering → CurriculumVersion → Units/Topics; Question → Topic/CO/BloomLevel; QuestionPublication → audience/availability; PaperVersion → Exam/SubjectOffering; PaperRelease → access rules.
Suggested read endpoints  GET /student/me, /student/subjects, /student/subjects/{id}/syllabus, /student/questions, /student/questions/{id}, /student/papers, /student/papers/{id}/view, /student/analytics.
Authorization  Server-side per-request student identity and enrollment checks; release-state checks for every question and paper; signed or authorized document access; no trust in client-side subject IDs.
Privacy and security  Secure sessions, least-privilege RBAC, encryption in transit, audit logs, download policy, rate limits, input validation, access-controlled files and institutional retention rules.
Integration  Reuse existing academic master data, approved question bank, syllabus mapping, exam metadata, paper release workflow and PostgreSQL schema; add student-specific read models and publication rules without duplicating academic source records.
34 / Acceptance Criteria, Rollout and Content Governance
Acceptance 1  A logged-in student sees only enrolled subjects for the selected current academic term.
Acceptance 2  Subject → unit → topic drill-down shows correct syllabus version and published question counts.
Acceptance 3  Question filters return correct marks, type, difficulty, Bloom’s and CO metadata; answers obey publication policy.
Acceptance 4  Mid-Term and End-Semester archives exclude unreleased, future or unauthorized papers even when a URL is guessed.
Acceptance 5  Analytics distinguish question coverage from student performance and handle missing mapping data without displaying false zeros.
Acceptance 6  Responsive screens, keyboard navigation, accessible labels, loading/empty/error states and reasonable performance with large question banks.
Phase 1  Identity and enrollment, overview, subjects, syllabus PDF, published question bank and authorized past papers.
Phase 2  Advanced Bloom’s/CO analytics, notifications, bookmarks and richer content previews.
Phase 3  Optional practice attempts, personalized recommendations and measured readiness analytics, subject to validation and academic policy.
Claims policy  Use proposed, designed or planned language for features not deployed. Avoid unverified accuracy percentages, autonomous quality guarantees, accreditation claims or invented customer testimonials.
